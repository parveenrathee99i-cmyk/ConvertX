Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Image Processing

At ConvertX, your privacy comes first. All image conversions, compressions, and resizing happen directly inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your files are never uploaded to any external server.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or view the images you convert. Once you close or refresh the browser tab, your processed images are cleared from browser memory.

3. Local Storage

We only use your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode).
