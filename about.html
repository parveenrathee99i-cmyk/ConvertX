About ConvertX

ConvertX is a free, fast, and privacy-focused online file utility built to make everyday document and image conversions effortless—without forcing you to upload sensitive files to unknown servers.

What You Can Do With ConvertX

  • All-in-One File Conversion: Convert between PDF, Word (.docx), JPG, PNG, and WebP formats in seconds.
  • Dedicated Image Resizer: Resize images to exact pixel dimensions with automatic aspect ratio locking and smart compression.
  • Batch Processing & ZIP Export: Convert or resize multiple files at once and download them together in a single ZIP archive.
  • 100% Browser-Based Privacy: Every file is processed locally on your device—nothing is ever stored or uploaded to any server.
