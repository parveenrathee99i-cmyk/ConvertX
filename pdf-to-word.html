How to Convert PDF to Word?

  1. Select a PDF document.

About PDF to Word Conversion

ConvertX extracts selectable text from PDF pages and places it into a Word document. Complex layouts, tables, images and scanned PDFs may not be preserved accurately. Scanned PDFs may require OCR before their text can be extracted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF to Word conversion free?

Yes, this converter is free to use.

Can I edit the Word document?

Yes, the downloaded DOCX file can be edited in compatible word-processing software.

Will formatting stay the same?

Not always. This basic converter extracts text, so complex formatting may not be preserved.
