How to Convert PDF to Word?

  1. Select a PDF file from your device.

About PDF to Word Conversion

ConvertX extracts selectable text from PDF pages and places it into a Word document. The conversion runs in your browser. Complex layouts, tables, images and scanned PDFs may not be preserved accurately. Scanned PDFs may require OCR before their text can be extracted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I edit the converted Word file?

Yes, the downloaded DOCX file can be opened and edited in compatible word-processing software.

Not always. This basic converter extracts text, so complex formatting and images may not be preserved.
